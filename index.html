Government support

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

India's central rooftop solar subsidy is filed alongside your DISCOM net-metering application — we handle both together.

Up to ₹78,000 central subsidy, credited straight to your bank account

Households can claim up to 300 units of free electricity a month under the scheme. Subsidy is disbursed after DISCOM commissioning and typically lands within 4–6 weeks — we track the application status until it's paid out.

How an install actually runs

Five stages, one point of contact

This is a genuine sequence — each stage depends on DISCOM sign-off from the one before it.

01

Site survey & load study

Roof orientation, shading, structural check and 12-month consumption analysis to size the system correctly.

02

Design & proposal

Panel layout, inverter selection and a line-item quote, including the subsidy you're eligible for.

03

DISCOM application & installation

Net-metering paperwork filed with your DISCOM while our crew installs racking, panels, wiring and the inverter.

04

Inspection & net-meter fitting

DISCOM inspects the installation and swaps in a bi-directional meter that records what you export.

05

Commissioning & subsidy claim

System switched on, generation dashboard handed over, and your PM Surya Ghar subsidy claim submitted.
